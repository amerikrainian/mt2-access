# Introduction

Monster Train 2 Accessibility is a screen reader and keyboard/controller accessibility mod for Monster Train 2. It adds spoken output, its own focus navigation over the game's screens, buffers of detail for whatever is focused, and announcements of what happens in battle.

## Features

- Speech output through Prism, with support for common screen readers and speech backends.
- Keyboard and controller navigation for the menus, run setup, the map, battles, rewards, shops, story events, challenges, Soul Savior, the Logbook and the rest of the game's screens.
- A key help (F1) that lists the keys that do something where you are, and runs them.
- Buffers holding a focused control's full detail, the game's events and the units' quotes.
- Announcements of battle events: turns and phases, ember, gold and forge points, the pyre, artifacts triggering, units spawning, moving, taking damage, gaining statuses and dying.
- Sounds where speech would be slow, starting with a beep when focus crosses between your units and the enemy's.
- The mod's own words in every language the game offers, following the game's language.
